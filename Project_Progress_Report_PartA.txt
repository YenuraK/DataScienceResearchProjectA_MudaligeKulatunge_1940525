In this work, you will record your progress to date. W e recommend employing this LaT eX template
 (https://protect-au.mimecast.com/s/xkC7CE8wo2IlXqyyhw30HS?domain=overleaf.com) , or following

Discuss your particular project and goals with your supervisor to decide how to structure your report.
When we mark your report, we will aim to provide feedback that guides you to improve your report
writing and analysis. 
Specific recommendations
1. Take the time to explain your work. For each figure/code/result: why was this important enough to
include? Describe what the figure/etc shows. Explain what the results mean. Remember that we can
only mark the understanding that you demonstrate in the report. 
2. Include figures! Use captions in the figure to point out which part of the report the figure is relevant
to, and make sure to reference those figures in the corresponding section of the report. 
 
The project report has a word limit of 6000 words . We encourage you to keep the introduction
sections brief; we have already read those. Most of the report should be about your work.
